36

Notifications fall out of the cast — nothing extra to wire per step. The slot a role sits in is its notification tier (in settings.notification_rules). 41

51

53

A role = what you can do with a grievance. It's the hinge the whole GRM turns on: a position type maps to a role (the matrix), a workflow step binds a role (who owns/escalates each level), an officer holds a role, and the role grants permissions. Change the role and you change what that safeguards officer can actually do. doc 11 §3 · doc 16 §4

32

One plain vocabulary, no "archetype." The role's "job in a step" uses the exact same words as the step cast — Handles it / Oversees / Kept informed / Can view (GRC panel & SEAH handler are "Handles it" specialisations) — and sets a starting permission set, then fine-tune in plain groups (Cases · GRC · SEAH · Reports). 33

Usage guard rails. Every row shows "Used on N steps · M officers"; a role held by officers but bound to no step warns (auto-assign would fail); delete is blocked while any step or officer references it. doc 11 §3.4 · F15

34

Two knobs, never conflated. The role's default area is only an invite-time suggestion; the actual scope each officer covers (country-wide vs one package) is set per person in their scope, not on the role. doc 11 §3.2

35

An org_admin (own track) creates and edits roles; project_admin and officer_admin only assign them (can't mint N incompatible roles); system/TOR roles' permissions are super_admin-only. The Roles surface is catalog hygiene — steps (Frame 04) are where roles are consumed. doc 11 §3.3/§3.5

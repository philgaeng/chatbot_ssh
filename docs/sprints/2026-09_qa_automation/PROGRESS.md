### ⚠ Tier 2 of QA-04c — the decision the ticket asks for *before* it starts

QA-04c says: *"Say so in `PROGRESS.md` before starting: if that pushes the sprint past what is
wanted, tier 2 is the part to split into its own ticket."* This is that note, written with tier 1
finished and measured.

**What tier 1 cost:** well under its 1–1.5 d estimate — the harness (04a) and the smoke table (04b)
did most of the work, and the flows reuse their fixtures. **What tier 2 would cost is not the same
shape.** Tier 1 was five flows against machinery that already existed. Tier 2 is five *groups*
(ticket actions · queue & search · settings · reports · mobile), each needing its own setup, and the
settings group alone spans eight sub-tabs.

⭐ **Two things learned in tier 1 that change how tier 2 should be priced.** Driving a flow **finds
defects at a rate reading code does not** — three of the five tier-1 flows turned up a finding
(`GRM-073`, `GRM-074`, and `GRM-071` from 04b's fixture work), and each one cost time to verify,
file and, where it blocked the flow, fix. That is the ticket doing its job, not overrun, but it
means tier 2's ~2–3 d is a **testing** estimate with no allowance for what it uncovers. And several
tier-2 flows will hit the same wall as `GRM-074`: a seeded database that cannot complete them.

**Recommendation: split tier 2 into its own ticket** and schedule it after QA-04d and QA-05. Tier 1
is what the harness had to prove; tier 2 is coverage, it splits cleanly (one spec file per group),
and it does not block the CI gate. **Owner's call** — nothing about it is urgent enough to overrule
the sprint's shape.
